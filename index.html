Research Information and Data Services
Collaborative Readiness Assessment

This assessment collaboratively identifies the research information and data services that support sponsored research across an institution. By building shared awareness, institutions can scale the services with the greatest impact and ensure they remain financially viable and sustainable.

A shared understanding strengthens compliance and promotes long-term financial stability by ensuring investments in research information and data services are accurately recognized, appropriately accounted for, and sustainably resourced.

The results are a conversation starter, not a directive: a shared basis for the library, research administration, and costing/finance to create cost transparency and align on indirect cost pool structure and grant charging decisions.

This initiative is based on the Research Information and Data Service Inventory (Craiglow & LaFlamme, ARL).

Any member of an institution can start the assessment. After all three perspectives are complete, see your outcomes report.
