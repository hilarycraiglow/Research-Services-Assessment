Research Services Collaborative Readiness Assessment

Is your library, research office, and costing team ready to talk about cost?

Built on the Research Information and Data Service Inventory (Craiglow & LaFlamme, ARL), this assessment asks three audiences the same 22 services, in their own language, then compares the answers to surface where library investments are ready for cost transparency — and where they should expand.

Library

Library leadership & service managers

For each service: do you offer it, can you isolate its cost, and can you show that cost per project or researcher?

Research Administration

Sponsored programs & research administrators

For each service: is it valuable to your research strategy, does it support grant compliance, and could you charge a grant for it?

University Costing

Institutional costing & cost accounting teams

For each service: do you have a cost center for it, is it already in F&A recovery, and would you move it to direct charging?

Combining results across teams

Each stakeholder fills out their section on their own device. Use Export to save your answers as a file, and ask the other two teams to send you theirs. Import each file to see combined Outcomes.
