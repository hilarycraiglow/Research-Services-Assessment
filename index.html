Research Services Collaborative Readiness Assessment

Built on the Research Information and Data Service Inventory (Craiglow & LaFlamme, ARL), this self-assessment asks the library, research administration, and university costing the same 22 services — each in their own language — then compares the answers to surface where library investments are ready for cost transparency, and where they should expand. It is a directional readiness check, not an audit.

WHAT YOU GET

  • The same 22 services, asked three ways — library, research admin, costing.
  • An outcomes dashboard ranking cost-transparency potential and expansion priorities.
  • Export/import so each stakeholder can answer on their own device.
  • A shared, plain-language record of where the three teams agree — and don't.
LIBRARY

Library

Library leadership & service managers

For each service: do you offer it, can you isolate its cost, and can you show that cost per project or researcher?

RESEARCH ADMIN

Research Administration

Sponsored programs & research administrators

For each service: is it valuable to your research strategy, does it support grant compliance, and could you charge a grant for it?

UNIVERSITY COSTING

University Costing

Institutional costing & cost accounting teams

For each service: do you have a cost center for it, is it already in F&A recovery, and would you move it to direct charging?
