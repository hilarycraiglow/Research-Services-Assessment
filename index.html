Research Services Collaborative Readiness Assessment

This assessment is a collaborative effort between the library, research administration, and university finance and costing teams, built on the Research Information and Data Service Inventory (Craiglow & LaFlamme, ARL). It asks about 22 library services from different perspectives and brings the answers together to start a shared conversation about how the library supports the research enterprise and where that support could be made more financially visible.

The results are a conversation starter, not a directive: a shared basis for the library, research administration, and finance to align on library cost pool structure and grant charging decisions together. That shared understanding strengthens compliance and supports long-term institutional financial stability by ensuring library investments in research support are accurately recognized, appropriately accounted for, and sustainably resourced.
